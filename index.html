Case Study 01

Biyode

Industry
Health & Wellness Supplements
Platform
Shopify
Scope
Store Design · Landing Page · Product Page
The Objective

Design a premium, US-facing Shopify store for a Himalayan shilajit supplement brand — pairing an editorial, wellness-led homepage with a conversion-focused pack-and-pricing module built for direct response traffic.

biyode — homepage
Pack & pricing selector
Comparison table
What I Worked On
  • Homepage design & hero section
  • Trust bar & social proof placement
  • Pack / subscription pricing module
  • "Original vs low quality" comparison table
  • Reviews section layout
Design Thinking

01 — An olive-and-cream palette signals natural wellness rather than a clinical, pharma look.

02 — The pack selector isolates the pricing decision from general browsing, so the visitor makes one choice at a time.
